# Guarded affine sample-depth comparisons

Status: held native trials; guarded V3/V4 and approximate V5/V6 show no useful repeatable gain for the priority scenes. No production adoption.

The current [BMW cycle profile](../scene-msaa-rgb-alpha-split/validation/profile-bmw/candidate-report.txt) puts roughly 32% of cycles in general/small 4× rasterization, with additional winner-storage and capture costs. Sharing material RGB has already reduced shading, so another large BMW gain must address this work. Keep the four original sample positions and exact integer top-left coverage; propose a cheaper affine depth plane for rejection/acceptance of samples during an explicitly captured opaque scene.

Compute a conservative interval around the proposed depth, accounting for integer-to-float conversions, expression grouping and clamping. There is no floating recurrence: both tested variants evaluate the plane from the current exact integer edges. A comparison is decided cheaply only when incoming and stored intervals are strictly separated. Otherwise evaluate the existing exact expression for both candidates, including reconstructing the old winner's depth if it was stored provisionally. Preserve equality and original triangle order. Pre-scene framebuffer values remain exact; the prototype applies the same conservative interval width to them. Alpha-tested materials and unsupported ranges retain the existing kernel after materializing pending winners in their owned stripe.

After visibility is complete, reconstruct the original exact sample depths for final winners before subsequent GL drawing or framebuffer observation. The private implementation temporarily reuses the still-unfilled deferred color storage for three vertex depths; attribute generation fills colors afterwards. Record size and sample-buffer allocations stay unchanged. Both clipped and unclipped records retain the original unbiased edges. The stored provisional depth is an upper bound, so the existing maximum hierarchy stays conservative. Failure uses the existing sample/framebuffer restoration and hierarchy invalidation. A mixed legacy record is already exact and must not interpret its colors as vertex depths.

This is an own proposal grounded in the [existing sample-depth expression and coverage kernel](../../libsoftgl/src/raster_msaa_impl.h), [captured winner records and rollback](../../libsoftgl/src/scene_visibility.c), [hierarchical-depth margins](../../libsoftgl/src/raster_hz.h), and [independent edge-arithmetic fixtures](../scene-msaa-rebased-edges/arithmetic_contract.c). It is not a reduced sample count, neighboring-pixel shader, mesh LOD or temporal cache. A simpler approximate depth plane without a proven comparison guard does not meet this experiment's correctness target.

Parent: `52aff7bda7d29f5af511b2938b02647d4025e7c1`, Clang 22.1.8 and SIMD128 only. The frozen control has exactly the parent's engine and wrapper sources. The first generator failed to compile because the worker handle is a `void *`; V2 built, then the positions contract found that final reconstruction read the cleared `c->scene_visibility`. V3 passes the frame object directly during final reconstruction and handles exact legacy records separately. Failed sources, recipes, logs and the signal receipt are retained.

V3 uses `b0*(z0-z2)+b1*(z1-z2)+z2`, a provisional upper offset of `2^-18` and interval width twice that value. V4 constructs coefficients directly from raw integer edges; the rebased kernel uses its exact coarse/remainder split, deferring the expensive original float conversions until an uncertain comparison. V4 uses `2^-19`, plus a uniform-winner reconstruction shortcut. Coverage, selected shading points and exact fallback expressions retain their original grouping.

The measured-library native contracts pass in both V3 and V4: 162 canonical full-plane pairs, 18 mixed captured alpha/opaque pairs, hint/admission/tiny-sample and rollback controls, a farther ordinary draw after six rollbacks, and 372 serial/worker plane pairs. Four million independent weighted intervals pass for each; an additional four million **raw/coarse-edge** intervals validate V4's distinct coefficient formulation. All **108 original-model views** in off/2×/4× match every exported RGBA/depth/stencil/sample-plane hash in V4. Native library and timed-driver disassembly confirms SIMD128 throughout. These finite numeric checks are empirical evidence, not an exhaustive proof of all floating inputs. Complete interval proof and actual WASM/sanitizer/browser adoption gates were not pursued without a useful gain.

One balanced BMW 4× AB/BA block, 60 warmup/30 rotating frames, 640×360 and caller plus three helpers: V3 **16.440158 → 18.122491 ms (+10.23% frame time)**; V4 **16.339642 → 17.596086 ms (+7.69%)**. Final angle-160 RGB is exact. These are screens, with every accepted/rejected attempt retained; no gain is established. The following additional scenes assess the more heavily weighted complex workloads.

The all-scene V4 screen gives Bistro +9.12%, Sponza +36.14% and T-80 +6.01% frame time. No repeat acceptance or production gates follow these regressions.

New BMW cycle profiles of the exact control and V3, enabled only after import and warmup, identify the additional work: `scene_guarded_exact_old` 7.66%, materialization 1.00%, and admission checks 1.19% of candidate user cycles. Total sampled event counts are approximately 13.84 versus 15.52 billion, with no lost samples. Those proportions explain a direction to improve; they are not a separate FPS gate.

V5 is a **private approximation control**: direct raw/coarse-edge depth planes with no interval guard, provisional bias or final reconstruction. It preserves four original coverage samples and full shading/assets, but may change depth rounding, tied winners and RGB. It cannot claim the guarded variants' exactness. Its purpose is to measure the potential savings before spending more work on exact reconstruction. Assess actual covered-depth masks and per-view errors before considering an explicit opt-in quality profile; no generic GL or test tolerance has been changed.

V6 isolates the direct plane to the rebased large-triangle kernel and removes its inner-loop eligibility branch and original weight calculation. The small/alpha kernels retain the parent's arithmetic. In first 4× screens (BMW/T-80/Sponza/Bistro), V5 changes time −0.74/+4.53/+25.55/−1.25%; V6 −6.54/−3.70/+24.29/+0.73%. V6's BMW median is inflated by a retained 17.954 ms baseline observation; its candidate stays around 16.1 ms, close to other control observations. No robust BMW gain is established from this screen.

The Sponza paired-resident slowdown is not reproduced by the separately launched cycle profile: control/direct V6 use about 33.48/33.44 billion cycles; profiled frame times are 33.529/33.302 ms. An independent **three-block identical-binary** Sponza campaign gives 32.693/33.368 ms (+2.07%), with identical images. It does not demonstrate a fixed 25% role bias. Retain this inconsistency instead of assigning the screen's whole slowdown to the arithmetic. Scene counters at two views differ by at most one shading packet: the expensive result is not explained by many more shaded pixels. The sampled profile is diagnostic and does not replace unprofiled repeated AB/BA acceptance.

V5's **36 native 4× view assessments** keep every covered-depth mask and stencil exact. Worst mean RGB error is 0.00718/255 (Bistro), with sparse local differences up to 113/255. BMW/T-80 maximum sample-depth change is one float ulp; Bistro/Sponza reach 9.42e-6/2.59e-5 because different nearby/cutout winners can be selected, not just rounding the same winner. Alpha bytes were not independently exported by this diagnostic driver. Its exploratory 2e-6 depth budget is exceeded in those scenes; that is a diagnostic result, not a new user quality requirement or a loosened repository comparator. No gain justifies introducing a new quality profile.

V6's separate **36-view assessment** also retains covered-depth masks and stencil. Maximum depth error is 1.79e-7 in Bistro and 2.59e-5 in Sponza; worst mean RGB errors are 0.00621/255 and 0.000266/255. Sparse local changes still reach 113/255 and 50/255. BMW/T-80 mean RGB errors are 0.0000651/255 and 0.0000217/255, with local maxima 16/255 and 3/255. This assessment does not independently export alpha either. Sponza exceeds the exploratory depth budget; no approximation is adopted.
